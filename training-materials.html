WRITING SAMPLE 04 · TRAINING MATERIALS

Training Module: Writing Effective Knowledge-Base Articles

A sample instructor-led training module combining objectives, examples, activities, and a knowledge check.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the user’s primary task.
  • Choose an appropriate documentation structure.
  • Write clear procedures.
  • Review content for accuracy and usability.

Module 1: Start with the user goal

Before drafting, describe what the reader needs to accomplish. This keeps the content focused on the task.

Activity

Rewrite: Account Management Features.

Example: How to Update Your Account Information.

Module 2: Build the procedure

Use numbered steps for sequential tasks and keep each step focused on one primary action.

Knowledge check

Question: What should a writer establish before choosing the structure of a how-to guide?

Answer: The user’s goal, audience, prerequisites, and task flow.
