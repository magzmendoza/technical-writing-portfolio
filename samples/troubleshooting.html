TECHNICAL WRITING SAMPLE

Fixing Common Setup Problems

A sample troubleshooting article.

Problem: Setup does not complete

Possible cause: A required setting is missing.

Solution: Review the setup form and complete every required field, then try again.

Problem: You cannot access a feature

Possible cause: Your account may not have the required permission.

Solution: Ask an administrator to review your role and permissions.

When to contact support

Contact support when the documented steps do not resolve the issue. Include the exact error message, the steps you followed, and relevant environment details.
