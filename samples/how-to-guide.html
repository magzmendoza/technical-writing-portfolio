How to Set Up a Product in 10 Minutes

A sample task-oriented how-to guide.

Before you begin

Make sure you have an active account and access to the product dashboard.

Step 1: Open the setup page

Sign in, open Settings, and select Setup.

Step 2: Enter your configuration

Enter the required information. Keep the values consistent with your organization's settings.

Step 3: Save and verify

Select Save. A confirmation message should appear. Run the verification check to confirm the setup is complete.

Troubleshooting

If verification fails, check the required fields first. If the problem continues, review your account permissions and contact support.
