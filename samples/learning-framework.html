WRITING SAMPLE 05 · LEARNING FRAMEWORK

Technical Learning Framework

A reusable framework for organizing technical learning experiences from foundational concepts through hands-on application.

Level 1 — Understand

Introduce essential terminology, concepts, product context, and the problem the technology solves.

Level 2 — Apply

Move learners into guided tasks using demonstrations and realistic scenarios.

Level 3 — Practice

Give learners an independent exercise that requires them to apply the concepts with limited guidance.

Level 4 — Validate

Use a knowledge check or practical assessment to confirm the learner can perform the workflow.

Level 5 — Reference

Provide quick-reference guides, FAQs, and troubleshooting content for later use.

Design principle

Each level answers a different question: What is it? How do I use it? Can I do it myself? Can I prove it? Where do I go for help?
